Calendar Paint

Automatic colors for Google Calendar. Write a rule like “title contains Gym → green” once, and every matching event stays colored, including every repeat of a recurring event.

Need help? Email support@enuf.ai. Tell us your Chrome version and which Calendar view you were using.

What it does

  • Colors events by title: contains, starts with, is exactly, regular expressions and more. The first matching rule wins.
  • Recurring events stay colored. Coloring one by hand colors the whole series by default.
  • Text switches to black or white automatically so it stays readable.
  • Open any event to see why it has its color, edit that rule, or keep Google's color for that event.
  • No sign-in, no account, no server. Everything runs in your browser.

Common questions

Colors are not showing.
Open the Calendar Paint window from the toolbar. The “Calendar Paint Health” panel says whether it is working. If it asks you to, reload your Google Calendar tab once.
Only some events are colored.
Rules run top to bottom and the first match wins. Open a rule to see a live preview of the events it matches. The rules list also says when a higher rule wins an event.
Why does this event have this color?
Click the event in Google Calendar. Under “Event color” it says which rule, manual color or default decided it, with links to edit the rule or keep Google's color.
All-day or recurring filters do not match.
These filters rely on Calendar's English interface. Rules without filters work in every language.
I want Google's colors back.
Use the switch at the top of the Calendar Paint window. Google Calendar looks exactly as before.
Do my colors show on my phone?
Not yet. Chrome extensions do not run in the Google Calendar mobile apps. Showing your colors on your phone is planned.
Does it read or send my calendar?
It reads the event titles Google Calendar already shows, only on calendar.google.com, and only to decide colors. Nothing leaves your browser. See the privacy policy.
